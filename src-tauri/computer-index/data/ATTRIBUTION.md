`cities.tsv` is a reduced derivative retaining GeoNames ID, name, ASCII name,
latitude, longitude and country code. Neighbourhoods (PPLX) are excluded so GPS resolves to a city rather than a city subdivision. No population or alternate-name columns.
The nearest listed city within 100 km is an approximate place, not an address;
more remote coordinates remain coordinates without an inferred Place.
